Who we are

Building a connected clean-energy future.

The proposed greenfield project brings key stages of the solar value chain under one integrated manufacturing ecosystem — from silicon wafers to solar cells and finished modules.

Alongside solar manufacturing, the project includes lithium-ion battery module assembly and a Battery Energy Storage System (BESS), supporting a broader energy transition.

Integrated production flow

One value chain. Four key stages.

The proposed solar manufacturing process connects specialist production stages with inspection and quality assurance.

01 — INGOT & WAFER

Shape the foundation

Crystal growth, ingot cropping, wafer slicing, cleaning and sorting.

02 — CELL PROCESSING

Engineer performance

Texturing, diffusion, deposition, passivation, metallization and testing.

03 — MODULE ASSEMBLY

Build the module

Cell stringing, lay-up, lamination, framing and junction-box installation.

04 — QUALITY & DISPATCH

Validate & deliver

EL inspection, flash testing, safety checks, packing and dispatch.

Beyond conventional solar

Future technology roadmap

TOPCon + Perovskite Tandem Solar Cells

The DPR proposes introducing tandem solar cell manufacturing in the next 2–3 years, targeting power conversion efficiency above 33%, with the aim of improving module performance and reducing electricity generation costs.

33%+ TARGET EFFICIENCY

Lithium-ion battery module assembly

Proposed assembly flow: incoming cell inspection, sorting and matching, module assembly, testing, battery pack assembly, final validation and dispatch. The DPR notes 12 acres for assembly and 120 employment opportunities.
